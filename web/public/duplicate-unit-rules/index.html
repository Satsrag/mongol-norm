01Scope and execution order

01.1

Run Step 1 (expansion) once before the control-character removal loop, and Step 2 (tail contraction) once after control-character removal stabilizes. Preserve all Mvs、Nirugu、Zwj、Zwnj units in place during both steps, then encode the resulting sequence as norm.

Nine written units or unit pairs render with identical ink in the reference Hudum font. Unifying them ensures that words with identical visual shapes produce the same unit sequence and the same canonical norm text.

01.2

Letter chains and position calculation

  1. Split the sequence at structural units (Mvs、Nirugu、Zwj、Zwnj) into contiguous letter chains. Process each letter chain independently.
  2. When computing a letter position (isol / init / medi / fina), a joiner (Nirugu or Zwj) immediately adjacent to the chain counts as a connection on that side, but does not belong to the letter chain and never acts as a bowed written unit.
01.3

Two steps across the control loop

  1. Step 1 (before control loop) — Expand single units (E1–E5): Scan each letter chain once from left to right. If a unit at its original chain position matches an expansion rule, replace it with the corresponding two units (O+A、A+A、N+N or O+O). Because Dd and Cr expand in all positions here, they never enter the ZWJ/ZWNJ drop tables.
  2. Step 2 (after control loop stabilizes) — Contract the tail pair (C1–C4): After control-character removal stabilizes, inspect only the last two units of each chain. If the chain ends with Aa, compute the position (isol or fina) that the merged single unit would occupy, and check the immediately preceding letter in the same chain. Perform at most one contraction per chain.

Step 1 only produces O, A, and N; Step 2 only produces A, Aa, B2, and G. Neither step nor control removal can create a new expansion target, and running Step 2 after control removal avoids contracting O+Aa or I+Aa across a redundant leading ZWJ.
